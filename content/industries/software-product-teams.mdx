## Common product-team needs

Product teams may need help with roadmap delivery, architecture choices, AI feature exploration, test coverage, performance work, or modernization while their own team continues serving customers.

## Engagement options

The engagement can be a focused delivery project, an embedded team, or ongoing engineering partnership with clear ownership and review checkpoints.
