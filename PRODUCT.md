# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Primary: business owners and decision-makers in established businesses who have an identifiable business problem and are evaluating software development, automation, integration, or modernization.
- Secondary: startup founders seeking a software product; operations managers seeking less manual work and connected systems; technology decision-makers evaluating an external engineering partner or ongoing support.
- The website should lead with business outcomes and keep all five audiences able to find relevant capabilities.

## Product Purpose

Archex Craft is a technology-services website for helping business decision-makers understand the company's software capabilities and start a project conversation. The intended outcome is an approachable, trustworthy explanation of what Archex Craft can build or improve and an easy next step for an interested prospect.

## Positioning

The owner-approved strategic direction is to present Archex Craft as a business-focused technology partner that helps companies build software, automate operations, and improve existing systems.

No unique competitive differentiator is confirmed. Delivery methods and service claims found in the repository are evidence of the current offer, not proof of a distinct market position. Any differentiator must remain a hypothesis until the owner confirms it.

## Operating Context

Prospective clients compare service providers, arrive with different levels of technical knowledge, and may know the business problem without knowing which technology or service they need. Some visitors are evaluating new-product development; others need to reduce manual work, connect existing systems, modernize software, or find ongoing engineering support.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The checked-in service catalogue has 22 offerings across AI and machine learning, web and CMS, ecommerce, mobile applications, and software development. Separate content also describes application modernization, AI integration, and custom software development.
- The site describes delivery/process content, example work, industries, insights, engagement models, and enquiry routes. The presence of a service page is not proof of a client result or credential.
- The source repository supports MDX and TypeScript content, with an environment-configurable database content path. The production content source is not verified here.
- The enquiry implementation supports optional Supabase lead storage, Resend notification, and Turnstile validation. Production configuration and delivery behavior require deployment-side verification.
- Do not imply a dedicated broad automation or integration service beyond the documented catalogue without owner confirmation.
- Do not invent client work, testimonials, measurable outcomes, certifications, team credentials, company history, or delivery guarantees.
- URLs and existing application behavior remain constraints during the research/planning stage; any future migration requires a documented SEO redirect plan.

## Brand Commitments

- Visible website brand: Archex Craft.
- Browser title brand: Archex.
- Canonical domain: `https://www.archexweb.in`.
- Communicate in plain language suitable for non-technical business decision-makers. Do not present technical capability as the visitor's required entry point.

## Evidence on Hand

- The repository contains two work entries marked illustrative, not verified completed-client case studies: `content/work/ai-assisted-intake-workflow.mdx` and `content/work/legacy-operations-platform.mdx`.
- No approved portfolio of completed client projects, testimonials, measurable outcomes, or certifications was supplied for marketing use.
- The code and content document service categories, example workflows, process descriptions, and technical capability statements. Treat these as the current offer and editorial claims, not independent proof of results.
- Company-specific differentiator and approved customer evidence remain open owner decisions.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with the business problem and desired outcome; explain technology in context.
2. Help visitors identify a useful next step even when they are unsure which service applies.
3. Make every credibility claim traceable to approved evidence; label illustrative material clearly.
4. Keep primary and secondary audiences discoverable without making every technical term part of the main navigation.
5. Make the enquiry path understandable, accessible, and clear about what happens next.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The redesign should target WCAG 2.1 AA. The current implementation needs manual keyboard and assistive-technology verification; do not describe it as conformant based only on static inspection.
